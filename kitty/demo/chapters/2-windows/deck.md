Windows, panes and tabs
=======================

Everything here is a real keypress:
watch the badge at the bottom.

<!-- end_slide -->

<!-- jump_to_middle -->

A new pane, same directory
==========================

⇧⌘↩ splits off a shell
that starts where you are.

<!-- end_slide -->

<!-- jump_to_middle -->

Move focus: ⌘ and an arrow
==========================

⌘← ⌘→ ⌘↑ ⌘↓

The focused pane glows; the rest dim.

<!-- end_slide -->

<!-- jump_to_middle -->

Move the pane itself
====================

B has focus. ⇧⌘← moves it
back one place: B and A swap.

<!-- end_slide -->

<!-- jump_to_middle -->

…and forward again
==================

⇧⌘→ moves B forward one
place: back below A.

<!-- end_slide -->

<!-- jump_to_middle -->

Layouts
=======

⌘F fat · ⌘G grid
⌘S stack · ⌘L tall

<!-- end_slide -->

<!-- jump_to_middle -->

Zoom one pane
=============

⌃⌥Z fills the tab with
the focused pane, and back.

<!-- end_slide -->

<!-- jump_to_middle -->

A pane to its own tab
=====================

⇧⌘↑ sends the focused pane
to a new tab (⇧⌘↓ asks where).

<!-- end_slide -->

<!-- jump_to_middle -->

Switch tabs
===========

⌃⇥ and ⌃⇧⇥.

<!-- end_slide -->

<!-- jump_to_middle -->

Panes, layouts and tabs
=======================

* ⇧⌘↩: new pane, same dir
* ⌘← ⌘→ ⌘↑ ⌘↓: move focus
* ⇧⌘← / ⇧⌘→: move the pane
* ⌘T / ⌘F / ⌘S / ⌘G: layouts
* ⌃⌥Z: zoom
* ⇧⌘↑: pane to a new tab
* ⌃⇥ / ⌃⇧⇥: switch tabs
